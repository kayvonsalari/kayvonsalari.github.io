Content Intelligence

LinkedIn Post Generator

An AI writing team where nothing gets shared until a critic is happy.

01The problem

AI can write a post in seconds, but the first draft is often plain, too long, or a little wrong. Someone still has to edit it, and that editing takes most of the time.

02What it does

The system works like a small newsroom. A researcher gathers material on the topic, a writer drafts the post, and a critic scores the draft against a fixed quality checklist. If the draft is not good enough, it goes back to the writer with the critic’s notes. This repeats until the draft passes.

03How it works

A supervisor agent runs the loop and decides who works next. The critic is meant to be strict: its job is to find what is wrong, not to agree. That built in disagreement helps improve the quality, like a good editor helping a writer.

04What came out of it

Posts that need far less editing by hand. More useful still, a simple pattern that works for any AI written content, from reports to customer letters: let one agent write, let another check, and only release what passes.
