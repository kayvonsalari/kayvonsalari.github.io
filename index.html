Investment Intelligence · Multi Strategy
Aristos Council: Equity Research You Can Audit

An equity research system based on one idea: AI you can audit. A company is compared with peers from about 25,000 listed companies and checked with a set of stock strategies, each asking one simple question: cheap and good, growing at a fair price, steady income, durable business. A separate check asks whether the profits are real. The result is fully deterministic: the maths decides, the AI writes, and a fact checker reads every sentence against the numbers. Every strategy has a ten year backtest record per industry, and a blind three model judge panel gives a second opinion with no vote. Public repo, more than 5,000 tests.

Financial Services · Platform Engineering
Governed Document to Decision Agent on Databricks

Mortgage paperwork goes in; six figures come out, each tied to the document it came from, with anything that does not add up flagged. The decision never touches the model: a Unity Catalog SQL function applies the lending rules from a policy table, and anything short of a clean pass is sent to a person with a drafted case note. I then tested it four ways. Three attacks failed; a note hidden in a real payslip fooled the AI on the first try. The README is a clear field report on why, and on the code checks that would stop it. Served as an MLflow endpoint and traced end to end.

Sports Analytics · Agent Protocol
Tennis Oracle — Deterministic Match Prediction

Betting markets price the top of the game well and ignore the bottom entirely. Tennis Oracle rates every ATP and WTA player from a million matches — tour, Challenger and ITF — and predicts at 65% where the market sits at 68%, but at 70% on women's ITF events no bookmaker prices at all. Verdicts come from surface-adjusted Glicko-2; the LLM only narrates. Speaks the A2A agent protocol, and a blind test of 197 matches showed the model analyst losing to the ratings by 7.6 points, which is why it narrates.
