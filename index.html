Investment Intelligence · Multi-Strategy
Aristos Council — Multi-Agent Equity Research

Aristos Council — an equity research system built on one idea: AI you can audit. Plenty of tools pick stocks. I built one that shows its work: every verdict is reproducible from a frozen data record months later, byte for byte; bad or missing data is refused (“not evaluated”) rather than silently filled; the LLM only narrates — a deterministic engine decides, and an automated fact-checker audits every sentence of the narration against the numbers, stamping warnings on anything that doesn't match; and single-name views quote the dated verdict-of-record rather than improvising a fresh opinion. The result is an agentic AI system designed the way regulated industries need them: deterministic where it matters, generative where it helps, and checkable everywhere. Public repo, 850+ tests, CI, cloud-agent development pipeline.
