Investment Intelligence · Multi Strategy
Aristos Council: Equity Research You Can Audit

A stock research tool that uses fixed rules for every buy, hold or sell call, while AI only writes the explanation. Each company is compared with peers from about 25,000 listed companies and checked against a set of simple strategies. A fact checker reads every sentence against the numbers, and every strategy has a ten year backtest record.
